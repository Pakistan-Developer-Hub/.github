## 🌍 Who We Are

**Pakistan Developer Hub (PDH)** is a community-driven initiative
dedicated to empowering students, aspiring developers,
software engineers, freelancers, and technology enthusiasts.

We believe that learning becomes more meaningful when
knowledge is transformed into practical experience.

PDH provides a collaborative environment where individuals
can exchange ideas, explore emerging technologies, contribute
to projects, and develop the skills needed to succeed
in the global technology industry.

Our community welcomes people at every stage of their
development journey — from beginners to experienced developers.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

To empower Pakistani learners and developers through
practical education, project-based learning, open-source
collaboration, knowledge sharing, and professional development.

## 🚀 Our Vision

To build a trusted and inclusive developer ecosystem in Pakistan
that connects talent, encourages innovation, creates meaningful
opportunities, and prepares individuals for the future
of technology.
